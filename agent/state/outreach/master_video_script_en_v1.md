Format: vertical 9:16, about 36-38 seconds, about 95 spoken words, big captions carry the whole meaning (WhatsApp plays it muted first), voice read by a REAL person (owner or team member), calm respectful tone, 2D illustration with a phone and chat bubbles. Same video for all English leads; only the on-screen slots change per lead:
`{PROJECT}` project name, `{CITY}`, `{TIME}` an example night time (e.g. 10:47 PM), `{BHK1}` `{BHK2}` the chips (NB Legacy: 4 BHK / 5 BHK; Pride: 3 BHK / 4 BHK; Brij: 2 BHK / 3 BHK; Elenza: 2 BHK / 3 BHK). Buyers are drawn as a neutral family, not a "young couple". No ad-rupee coin, no prices, no numbers or percentages, no AI/bot/CRM words, never "booked" or "registered" (RERA). The animated chats carry a small "Example" tag.

## Why this version (4 developer personas judged 3 drafts)
Scores average understood / would-reply / trust: night-story 8.5 / 5.8 / 6.0, owner-calm 6.5 / 4.5 / 7.2, time-roi 7.2 / 4.2 / 5.8. The winner (one buyer's night) is the base; it takes the calm owner tone from the second and drops the ad-rupee framing of the third (the luxury owner hated it). Changes made from their feedback: shorter (about 36-38 s instead of 48); the "Without automatic reply" phone and the cold-chat scene are gone (they read as blaming the team); the split label is neutral; says it works with all your ads and forms on your own number; a real company name and city in the first seconds; the ask is specific ("free demo for your project, no obligation"); chips match the project.
Not taken: a real proof point (a live client result): we have none yet, so the voice-over by a named real person and the clear ask carry the trust; see the open items.

## Open items for the owner (decisions, nothing assumed silently)
1. **Is the demo free?** The script says "free, with no obligation": confirm, or change the line.
2. **What does "Yes, send the demo" send?** Proposed: a short live sample chat for {PROJECT} plus the page link, and a 10-minute call ONLY if they ask. (The old line "nobody calls unless you ask" was removed because Day 2-3 follow-ups exist.)
3. **Forms:** the script says it works with their ad forms too (Elenza uses a Facebook form). Confirm Ivinfotech can connect ad forms to the WhatsApp reply; if not, delete "and forms" in scene 2.
4. **Proof:** if a real screen recording of a live instant reply (our own system, with a visible timer) can be made, use it in scene 1 or 3 instead of the animation.
5. **Who records the voice** (owner or a team member), a calm natural pace, no music over the voice.
