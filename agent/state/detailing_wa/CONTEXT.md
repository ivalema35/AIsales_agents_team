## Delivery reality (campaign 1) - do not ignore
A cold MARKETING template to a number that never opted in is a Meta-policy risk and is often NOT delivered (error 131049 "healthy ecosystem"): campaign 1 = 2 of 7 delivered. Rules the owner accepted: owner-approved per lead, one lead at a time, wait ~60 s and read the status before the next send, 3 consecutive non-deliveries = cold templates OFF, never retry the same number the same day, STOP honoured at once. Default safer routes = a human 1:1 WhatsApp (the owner's own number) or a lead-initiated chat. Meta new-portfolio cap 250 unique recipients / 24 h.
