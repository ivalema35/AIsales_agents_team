Video upar, message neeche, ek hi message me jayega aur caption ke links tap ho jaate hain. Apna naam jodna ho to 'Namaste ... team,' ke baad 'Main <naam>' likh do (Nexus360 wale message me naam tha).

**Click-to-send link:** tap karo, chat khulta hai aur message text-box me aa jata hai (video alag attach karna padega: text cut karo, video attach karo, caption me paste karo). Page link ke preview card me ab video ka poster + play button dikhta hai.
