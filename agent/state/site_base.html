Five small leaks that cost site visits

Each one is easy to fix once enquiries have a proper home.

The leak

Enquiries get buried

A new buyer writes at night and sits under dozens of unread chats.

The fix

A lead board with reminders

Every enquiry becomes a card with a status, and the team sees who needs a reply today.

The leak

Nobody knows which ad worked

Facebook, Instagram and reels all land in the same chat.

The fix

Source tracking

Each ad gets its own link, so every enquiry shows where it came from.

The leak

Follow-ups depend on memory

A notebook, or a promise to call back tomorrow.

The fix

An automatic first reply

The buyer gets the brochure and a visit link automatically, usually within seconds.

The leak

Buyers can't compare phases

Brochures are forwarded one by one and go out of date.

The fix

One project page

All phases, floor plans and location on one link you can use in every ad.

The leak

Visits are arranged by phone tag

Three calls just to fix one Sunday slot.

The fix

Pick-a-slot visit scheduling

Buyers choose a time; your team sees it on the board.
