Sources and limits

  • Register data: Chhattisgarh RERA public list, https://rera.cgstate.gov.in/Approved_project_List.aspx, one request on 2026-10-20 10:36. No login, no CAPTCHA. The authorised person's name, phone numbers and e-mails on the register are not kept.
  • Funnel counts come from the saved summary of that run. The district split and the monthly columns are counted from the same saved list.
  • Small or medium is "3 or fewer registered projects", a stand-in. Customers are removed by name match only.
  • Sample: pipeline counts, the lead card, the phone message and the hand-off log are illustrations with invented numbers, not results or promises. The research columns in the builder table are real for the 4 builders we tested and empty for the rest. Real delivery on cold WhatsApp numbers is limited by Meta.
  • Owner confidence labels are a proposal. In an earlier round in another state, an owner or decision-maker was named for 6 of 9 contacted builders. In our 20 Oct test of 6 Chhattisgarh builders, an owner or decision-maker was named for 4 of 6 (all rated Likely), a business-published WhatsApp number was found for 1 of 6, and a verified direct owner WhatsApp number for 0 of 6. That is a small test, not a forecast.
  • Scope: this sample covers Chhattisgarh only. The WhatsApp line used to send is IV Infotech's.
