# What we understood about Nexus360 and its buyers

For your correction. IV Infotech, 20 October 2026. Based only on your public pages (nexus360.in, greyboard.in, sridharpatnaik.com). Please mark anything that is wrong or out of date.

**Nexus360.** An ERP and CRM for real-estate builders. On the site and purchase side: BOQ, indents, purchase orders with supplier quote comparison, inward receipts, and live stock and consumption by project. On the sales side: leads, follow-ups and site visits, the deal pipeline, payment schedules and collections. It also answers plain-English questions on live data, supports several companies, can be white-labelled, and each company runs on its own server. It began in 2020 for one customer, replacing diaries and Excel. Your pages say 20+ projects and 200+ users.

**Who we think buys it.** The owner or a partner signs. The accounts lead, the purchase or project head and the site and store team decide whether it is actually used. Likely triggers: a second or third project, a new registration, RERA reporting deadlines, stock or purchase leakage, a new finance or project head. Most builders today probably run on Tally, Excel, WhatsApp groups and site registers.

**Who we would look for.** In the public Chhattisgarh RERA register on 20 October 2026, about 376 private builders have a project marked ongoing, 179 of them in Raipur, Durg and Rajnandgaon. About 30% of those 179 have two or more ongoing projects. These are our own counts and are approximate. We would favour builders with 2 to 8 ongoing projects, a registration in the last 12 months, and apartment or commercial work. We would leave out government bodies, builders with a single plotted layout, and your existing customers (we would need your list). Madhya Pradesh is not automated, because its RERA site disallows bots.
