What we promise

  • Researched records for up to 50 builders, every fact with a source and date. Where we cannot find the owner, the record says Unknown.
  • A first message and at most one follow-up, only to published numbers, only after a person on our side has checked the record, always with a STOP option.
  • Every genuine reply forwarded to the person you name. We stop messaging a builder at once after any reply or STOP.
  • The read-only view, a CSV, and the Day 17 report.

What we do not promise

  • Replies, meetings, demos, customers or any reply rate. Our own first campaign: 9 contacted, no confirmed genuine reply; 2 of 7 cold WhatsApp messages arrived on time. Fact
  • An owner’s direct WhatsApp number. In our test of 6 builders an owner or decision-maker was named for 4 (none fully verified), a WhatsApp-capable published number was found for 1, a verified direct owner number for 0. Fact, small sample
  • That every builder can be messaged, that every message is delivered (Meta decides), or a reply-rate answer from 50 builders.
  • Madhya Pradesh: its RERA site disallows bots, so it is not automated.
