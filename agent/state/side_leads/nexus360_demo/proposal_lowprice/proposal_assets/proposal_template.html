Limits, compliance, terms and next steps

Limits

  • The real owner's direct number is often not public. In our test it was found for none of six, and a number wired for WhatsApp for one of six. A builder with no WhatsApp-capable published number is researched but not messaged in the pilot.
  • We do not promise replies, demos or customers. {{c1_sentence}}
  • Delivery depends on Meta. 2 of 7 of our own cold templates arrived on time.
  • Madhya Pradesh is not automated. Its RERA site's robots.txt disallows bots. We could ask MP RERA for permission or build a list by hand, later. Neither is in this proposal.
  • "Small or medium" is a stand-in until we use filed project cost and a group check. Director lookups are done by hand in the pilot; automating them needs a licence that is not priced here.
  • Correction to our first message. It quoted "92+ staff hours" and "₹23,000+ monthly overhead saved". We cannot source those figures, so please disregard them. The pilot is a 14-day test, not a finished deployment.

Compliance Risk summary, not legal advice

  • Public data only. Contact details only where the business published them (its own site, an ad or the RERA register), stored with source and date. No bought lists. Numbers are masked in reports.
  • Opt-out. Every message carries a STOP line. STOP is honoured and added to a suppression list.
  • Meta policy. WhatsApp expects opt-in. Messages to published numbers carry a risk of limits or a block. The account is ours, so we carry that risk.
  • DPDP Act 2023. Data made public by the person, or by someone legally bound to publish it, may fall outside the Act (section 3). Director names from third-party listings may not. Most duties start about May 2027. We follow the practice now: a notice on the page, takedown on request, a deletion date for unverified contact data. Ask your own counsel.
  • Sender. We recommend a separate +91 WhatsApp number with a clear display name, subject to Meta's approval and your permission, so a restriction does not reach our other lines.
