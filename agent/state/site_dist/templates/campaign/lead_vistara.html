What changes when replies are automatic

When every reply is typed by hand

  • A buyer who messages at night may wait until morning.
  • The same brochure message can get typed again and again.
  • Ready buyers and casual ones can look the same in the chat list.
  • Follow-ups can depend on someone remembering.

With IV Infotech

  • The buyer can get an automatic reply in about a minute, even at night.
  • The brochure and three quick questions go out by themselves.
  • One simple list shows buyers who picked a visit time first.
  • Polite reminders can go to buyers who agree to get them, for example on day 1, day 3 and day 7.

Questions you may have

What happens when I tap the button?

WhatsApp opens with a ready message. Press Send, and our automatic assistant replies with a short sample of this chat for Anahata Florenza, from our own number. Nothing is connected to your number or your ads. We use your number only to send the demo, reply to you and, if you say yes, follow up. A call happens only if you ask for one.

What does it cost?

The demo is free. If you want to go ahead, we give the full price in writing before you pay anything. WhatsApp's own message charges are separate and we show them to you.

Doesn't WhatsApp already have an auto-reply?

WhatsApp's own greeting is one fixed message. This sends a brochure, asks three quick questions, offers a site-visit time and lists who picked a visit time for your team.

Will buyers feel they are talking to a machine?

Buyers are told the first reply is automatic. They can ask for a person, and your team can take over any chat whenever it wants.

Which WhatsApp number does it use?

Normally the WhatsApp number your ads already use. We check this with you first, and nothing is switched on without your approval. For the demo we use our own number, not yours.

How do I stop hearing from you?

Reply STOP in the WhatsApp chat and we will stop messaging you. You can also email ronakpatel@ivinfotech.com.
