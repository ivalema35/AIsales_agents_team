Questions you may have

What happens when I tap the button?

WhatsApp opens with a ready message. Press Send, and we reply with a short sample of this chat for Elenza Gardens, from our own number. Nothing is connected to your number or your ads. We keep your number and message only to send the demo. A call happens only if you ask for one.

What does it cost?

The demo is free. If you want to go ahead, we give the full price in writing before you pay anything. WhatsApp's own message charges are separate and we show them to you.

Doesn't WhatsApp already have an auto-reply?

WhatsApp's own greeting is one fixed message. This sends your brochure, asks three quick questions, offers a site-visit time and shows your team who is ready.

What do I need to do?

Nothing technical. You share your brochure and your sales team's details. We set it up with you and support it after launch. Scope and price are shared before you decide.

Will buyers feel they are talking to a machine?

Buyers are told the first reply is automatic. They can ask for a person, and your team can take over any chat whenever it wants.

Which WhatsApp number does it use?

Normally the number your ads already point to. We check this with you first, and nothing is switched on without your approval. For the free demo we use our own number, not yours.

How do I stop hearing from you?

Reply STOP in the WhatsApp chat and we will stop messaging you. You can also email info@ivinfotech.com.
